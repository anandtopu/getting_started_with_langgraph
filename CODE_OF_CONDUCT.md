# Code of conduct

This course welcomes beginners and experienced contributors. Treat questions, mistakes, and different backgrounds with patience and respect.

Be specific and constructive when discussing code or teaching material. Critique behavior and ideas, not people. Give others room to learn. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish someone's private information, or share real customer data.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions, pause a discussion, or restrict participation when needed. Decisions should explain the relevant conduct and use the least disruptive effective response.

Report ordinary course problems through the repository's issue templates after publication. For a conduct concern, use a private contact channel offered on a maintainer's public profile; do not post personal or sensitive details in a public issue. This repository does not invent a reporting address before its maintainers establish one.

These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other community spaces associated with the course.
