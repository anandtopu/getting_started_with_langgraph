# Hints

## Hint 1

An adjuster collects reports from several specialists and signs only after the slowest specialist finishes. Compare the first differing field with the task's required behavior.

Do not treat arrival order as the public result order. Inspect the operation that owns this update or routing choice.

## Hint 3

The reference contract uses this operation: sorted(state["findings"]). Check every boundary input after the repair; a default-case pass alone is insufficient.
