# Worked design review

A swarm-style team routes according to the active specialist, without requiring a central supervisor on every transition. Its business budget is distinct from the graph recursion limit. This controlled four-role comparison measures calls and completion; scripted answers do not establish that the team is smarter than the one-call baseline.

- routing: choose active_role; this preserves the boundary exercised by the example.
- budget: choose explicit_call_count; this preserves the boundary exercised by the example.
- comparison: choose single_agent_baseline; this preserves the boundary exercised by the example.

Rejected alternative: combine all decisions in an unvalidated, mutable global state. It hides ownership and makes independent failure checks harder.
