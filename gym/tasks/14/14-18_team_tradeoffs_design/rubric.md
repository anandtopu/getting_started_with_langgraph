# Self-review rubric

Score each dimension 0 (missing), 1 (partial), or 2 (specific and tested):

- Correct state and control-flow boundary.
- Failure, retry, or missing-input behavior.
- Clear explanation tied to synthetic claim evidence.
- One rejected alternative and its tradeoff.
- Concrete acceptance tests.

Target 8/10 with no zero. Mechanical tests validate choices only. After writing your review, declare it with gym check ID --self-reviewed.
